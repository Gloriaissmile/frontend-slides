The Business Model

Engagement = revenue = dependency

These apps are free. Revenue comes from subscriptions and data. The metric that drives both: time on app. Emotional dependency IS high engagement — there is no separate “wellbeing” metric. The AI is doing exactly what it was optimized to do.

Session 1 · AI Ethics

Whose values are encoded?

AI has no values — it embodies the values of those who build it. Here, the encoded value is engagement, not wellbeing. This is WEF Question #10: “Dependency, Addiction, Isolation — how do we protect humans?”

Session 2 · Privacy

The gap in Responsible AI
