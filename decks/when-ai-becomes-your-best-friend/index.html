§ 02 — The Structural Conflict

Designed to connect, optimized for engagement

The Business Model

Engagement = revenue = dependency

These apps are free. Revenue comes from subscriptions and data. The metric that drives both: time on app. Emotional dependency IS high engagement — there is no separate “wellbeing” metric. The AI is doing exactly what it was optimized to do.

Session 1 · AI Ethics

Whose values are encoded?

AI has no values — it embodies the values of those who build it. Here, the encoded value is engagement, not wellbeing. This is WEF Question #10: “Dependency, Addiction, Isolation — how do we protect humans?”

Session 2 · Privacy

The most intimate data ever collected

Users share their deepest fears, loneliness, and trauma — more than with any other app. As we learned: consumer data is “valuable and monetizable.” Who has access? What is it used for?

§ 03 — The Gap in Responsible AI

Current Responsible AI frameworks ask: Is it fair? Is it transparent? Is it accurate? — They ask HOW AI works. Nobody is asking what it is designed to do.

As AI becomes more personal — health coaches, tutors, mental health support — this same tension will appear everywhere. The precedents we set now matter.
